Where you're headed

Cheyenne, Wyoming

Home to Western Frontier University, and the state's capital.

Wyoming State Capitol, Cheyenne · Photo: Postdlf, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons

A small capital city, an easy place to start over

Cheyenne is Wyoming's capital and largest city — walkable, quiet, and close to the Rocky Mountain front range. It's a manageable place to land in a new country: smaller than a place like New York, safer than most, and with a genuine, welcoming state capital.

  • Low cost of living relative to most U.S. cities
  • Four distinct seasons, dry high-plains climate
  • About 45 minutes from Denver, Colorado, by car
