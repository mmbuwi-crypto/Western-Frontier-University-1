How boarding works

Four stages, start to departure

Every application moves through the same four checkpoints, in order.

CHECK-IN

Submit application

Complete your profile and pay the 5,000 F CFA processing fee to lock in your file.

SCREENING

Document verification

Our team verifies transcripts, admission status, and identity documents.

BOARDING CALL

Interview & selection

Shortlisted applicants are interviewed; the selection committee finalizes the cohort.

DEPARTURE

Visa & enrollment support

Awarded scholars get help with visa paperwork, housing setup, and travel booking.
