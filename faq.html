Questions

Before you board

What is the 5,000 F CFA fee for?

It covers the cost of verifying your documents and processing your file. It funds administration only — it is not a source of scholarship funding, and it does not affect your chances of being selected.

Is the fee refundable?

No. It covers processing costs incurred whether or not you're selected. If cost is a barrier, reach out about a fee waiver before submitting.

Does paying the fee guarantee a scholarship?

No. Payment confirms your application has entered the review process — selection is based on eligibility, documents, and interview outcomes.

What does "partial travel" cover?

A fixed contribution toward one round-trip flight per academic year. The exact amount is published in the full program guide.

Can I apply if I haven't been admitted to a U.S. school yet?

Yes, if your admission application is already submitted and under review — you'll need to show proof of that application.
