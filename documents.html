Pack these

Required documents

Have these ready as PDF or clear photo scans before you start — the application can't be submitted without them.

01

Academic transcripts

Most recent two years, official or certified copies.

02

Proof of U.S. admission (or in-progress application)

Offer letter, or confirmation an application is under review.

03

Government ID or passport

Valid, unexpired, clearly legible.

04

Personal statement

Under 800 words, in your own words.

05

One recommendation letter

From a teacher, professor, or employer.

06

Proof of English proficiency

If your prior instruction wasn't in English.
