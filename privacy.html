Privacy & contact

How we handle your information

You'll be asked to submit a passport, transcripts, and other personal documents as part of this application. Here's what happens to them.

What we collect

Full name, contact details, school information, a personal statement, and the documents listed on the documents page — including a government ID or passport, academic transcripts, and a recommendation letter. If you pay the application fee, we also collect your mobile money transaction ID and a screenshot of that transaction as proof of payment.

✓
Who sees itYour file is visible to the admissions and verification staff processing scholarship applications, and to the selection committee if you're shortlisted. It is not sold, and it is not shared with third parties for marketing.
✓
How it's storedSubmissions are stored in our application processing system. Uploaded files (including your ID and transcripts) are only accessible to authorized staff.
✓
How long we keep itUnsuccessful applications are retained for a limited period for audit purposes, then deleted. Successful applicants' records are retained for the duration of their enrollment plus the standard retention period required for university academic records.
✓
Your rightsYou can ask us what information we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete an unsuccessful application. Contact us using the details below.

A note on the application fee

We will never ask you to send the application fee to a personal account, or to any number other than the one published on the application page. If anyone contacts you claiming to represent this program and asks you to pay a different number, or to pay by an unusual method, treat it as fraud and contact us directly using the details below before sending anything.
